# ExtraOK launch campaign

## Status

Not yet launched. Public deployment and a real checkout must be verified first. No channel activity may imply that a GitHub issue form is a checkout.

## Positioning

**Extra work should never depend on a vague text thread.** ExtraOK gives small contractors a clear, private, cross-platform approval and signed decision receipt without forcing either party into a full field-service suite or client account.

## Claims allowed

- Works in a modern browser and can be installed as a PWA.
- Project details remain in browser storage and private URL fragments; the static host does not receive them during normal use.
- Issued packages and decision receipts are cryptographically signed and checked for tampering.
- No analytics, ads, cookies, external scripts, or project-data sale.

## Claims prohibited

Do not call records legally binding, court-proof, dispute-proof, identity-verified, compliant in every state, encrypted end-to-end, or impossible to alter. Do not claim customers, revenue, testimonials, or savings without evidence.

## Initial lawful channels

1. Product repository and GitHub Pages landing page.
2. Search-oriented original guide: a neutral change-order approval checklist with jurisdiction caveats.
3. Product directories that allow owner submissions and do not require fabricated traction.
4. Contractor/bookkeeper/trade communities only where self-promotion is explicitly permitted; disclose maker affiliation.
5. Opt-in partner outreach to contractor educators and bookkeepers; no scraped lists or bulk unsolicited messages.

## Launch sequence and evidence

For each post/submission record date, exact URL, account, copy, disclosure, rules checked, read-back result, tagged destination, visits, activations, qualified replies, refunds, and payments. Begin with one small batch, verify every public write, observe results, then expand only channels showing qualified activity.
