Privacy without fine print.

The ExtraOK web app is designed so the operator does not receive your project records.

What stays on your device

Company details, client names, job descriptions, amounts, approval records, browser signing keys, backups, and license activation data are stored in your browser. Clearing site data can permanently delete them. Export backups regularly.

Private review links

Review packages and decision receipts are encoded after the # in the URL. Browsers do not send URL fragments in ordinary HTTP requests, so the static host does not receive that content. Anyone who receives a link can read its contents. Share links only through a trusted channel and revoke them operationally by issuing a replacement.

What this site does not use

  • No application account or project database
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, or third-party scripts
  • No cookies
  • No sale of customer or project data
  • No AI processing of project content

Hosting logs

The static hosting provider may receive ordinary web-server information such as IP address, requested path, browser type, and time. URL-fragment contents are not included in HTTP requests. Review the hosting provider’s privacy terms for its independent practices.

Purchases and support

Purchases use the active Stripe-hosted checkout. Stripe independently receives and processes the checkout email, payment, tax, fraud-prevention, and receipt information under its own policies; ExtraOK does not receive or store card details. Do not put client, job, payment, tax, or other sensitive information in a public GitHub issue. Payment providers and support channels process information under their own notices.

Security limits

Browser-generated signatures can detect changes to an issued package or receipt. They do not verify a person’s government identity, authority, or legal capacity. A lost device, compromised browser, exposed link, malicious extension, or cleared browser storage can defeat practical protections.

Your controls

You can export records, delete individual browser records, or clear all ExtraOK site data through browser settings. Because the app has no project database, the operator cannot restore local records.

Contact

Use the support link published on the official ExtraOK repository. Do not send secrets or unredacted customer documents.
